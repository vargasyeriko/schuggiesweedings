Tips to make this playlist your playlist

  • Swap in personal “you two” songs — whether it’s an indie track you both love or your first-date song.
  • Watch transitions — don’t go from heartbreak ballad straight into EDM; have a bridging track.
  • Consider tempo curves — start mellow, build to big energy, then wind down.
  • Lean into Scottish/folk flair — slip in versions with pipes, fiddle remixes or ceilidh covers.
  • Ask guests — include a “must-play” song request line on your RSVPs.

Why ceilidhs just work

Here’s the secret: ceilidhs aren’t about dancing perfectly. They’re about throwing yourself into it, laughing when you inevitably go the wrong way, and sharing the moment with the people around you.

  • Everyone dances with everyone — it’s the ultimate ice-breaker
  • The more people, the more fun (ceilidhs thrive on big crowds)
  • No one feels awkward because honestly? No one really knows what they’re doing 🤣
  • Perfect for weddings where you’ve got guests from all walks of life — ceilidhs connect them instantly

I’ve lost count of how many times I’ve seen complete strangers walk in… and leave the dancefloor as new pals.
