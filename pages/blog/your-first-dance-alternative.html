Ceilidh Dance – Your First Dance Alternative!

A first dance is a special moment in a wedding reception that’s all about the happy couple. It’s when they’re first introduced to their friends and family(day time & evening guests), it’s when they have their first chance to share how much they love each other with everyone special to them gathered together in one place. And it’s also the time for them to show off their skills on the dance floor.

The first dance tradition has been going strong since couples started getting married hundreds of years ago, but there are still some people who either cringe at the thought or just don’t want to do a traditional first dance at their own wedding. You would be surprised actually how many couples do cringe😲 Maybe you’ve always wanted something different? Or maybe you just don’t like dancing very much and the thought of a disco first dance fills you with dread!

A ceilidh (pronounced Kay-lea BTW) may well be a great solution for those who want to have a first-dance alternative at their wedding. Perfect for those who don’t want to do the first dance🤯. Great for those who “can’t dance”. Ideal for those who don’t want to miss out on the chance to mark the first dance but at the same time need some gentle encouragement and guidance when out on the dance floor!

A ceilidh is one of the most popular traditional dances in Scotland, Ireland, and other parts of the United Kingdom, and it’s also had a recent resurgence of popularity in North America. A ceilidh is a call-and-response type of folk dance, done without the need to plan ahead, know what you are doing or worry about making mistakes😍.

Can you skip the first dance?

Some people may believe that skipping the first dance at their own wedding is not possible. Remember, it’s your wedding. So do it your way! With a ceilidh, you have a great alternative and should a couple not feel comfortable, it’s definitely an option. However, first dances still add a sense of tradition and magic to the wedding day and a ceilidh can provide you with a sensational and unique first dance!

A Wonderful Alternative!

A ceilidh first-dance is an excellent alternative because it’s designed to be inclusive and engaging for all guests, getting them socially involved right from the start. Everyone can get involved and socialise while “ceilidhing” which is of course what we’re after at a great wedding. No-one will forget your wedding in a hurry with what is a first dance like no other

Too Shy to Dance?

A ceilidh is a great first-dance alternative for people who are too shy to dance in front of others. A lot of my couples really don’t feel comfortable being that sort of centre of attention. It’s ideal for those who don’t necessarily want three minutes of eyes on them – not everyone wants the limelight! It’s a great first-dance alternative for the shy and retiring because not only do they get to share that moment with their friends and family, but enjoy it in an environment where everyone is busy having fun.

A ceilidh brings all your guests together to celebrate you both as a couple

When given the chance, most people love to dance and a ceilidh is a fantastic first-dance alternative for those who want their first moments as newlyweds to be shared with everyone.

Have two left feet?

Not everyone in the marriage is built to waltz away their partner knowingly and successfully. Many folks struggle to manage their brain and feet at the same time. Trying to skate over the floor with such a partner is only going to lead to bruised toes and wounded pride. If you feel flustered it might be time to consider alternatives such as a Ceilidh. A ceilidh requires no knowledge of how to dance and it also gets all your guests involved.

A great way to kick-off the Evening Reception

A first dance is a very important part of the evening so choosing the correct first dance is crucial. A ceilidh first dance can set the tone for the rest of the evening. If you have a ceilidh first dance, all your family (both sides) and friends can join in on it with you which will lead to everyone having a great time.

It’s also a great first dance alternative for people who aren’t keen on the traditional first dance affair but would still like to mark their first dance as Spouses with friends and family.

A ceilidh brings together all your guests to celebrate you as a couple on your wedding day, as well as providing some fantastic first-dance alternatives that some brides and grooms might not have considered!

Unique Music

Traditional first dance songs are often used way too many times so providing this special moment with ceilidh music is so much fun. With a ceilidh wedding, you’ll experience lovely up-tempo music that will excite and delight your guests. This contemporary Celtic sound will have your guests dancing all night long.

Who could forget a first dance to the sound of a Scottish Fiddle vs a first dance song everyone has heard a thousand times?

Conclusion:

A first dance is a tradition that many couples consider an important part of their wedding day. However, not everyone wants to do the first dance themselves or have it be so formal and traditional.
