What exactly is a ceilidh?

A ceilidh (pronounced Kay-Lee) is a traditional Scottish social dance — think live music, group dances, and a caller (that’s me!) who guides everyone through each step. It’s less ‘formal dance’ and more ‘joyful chaos’. Everyone joins in, laughs, and has a great time, even if they’ve never danced before.

Do we need to know the dances beforehand?

Nope! That’s the beauty of it — no experience needed. I teach all the moves on the night, nice and easy. The aim isn’t perfection; it’s having a laugh and enjoying the energy.

What if our guests aren’t Scottish? Will they still enjoy it?

Absolutely. Ceilidhs are for everyone — they’re social, inclusive and universal. Whether your guests are from Glasgow, Galway or Gloucester, they’ll love it. In fact, most couples tell me their non-Scottish guests were the keenest dancers!

Can we still have a ceilidh if our venue is small?

Totally. I’ve hosted ceilidhs everywhere from village halls to castles to tipis. If there’s space to swing your arms, there’s space for a ceilidh! I tailor each event to your venue, so we make the most of what you’ve got.

Is a ceilidh suitable as a first dance alternative?

Definitely — and it’s becoming a big 2026 trend! Instead of a slow dance under pressure, kick off your evening with a ceilidh dance that gets everyone up and celebrating with you.
