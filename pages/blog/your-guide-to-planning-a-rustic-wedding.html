A rustic wedding remains a timeless choice for couples celebrating their love in a relaxed, natural setting

This theme, is perfect for those who adore the simplicity and beauty of the great outdoors,

has evolved to become more enchanting and versatile than ever🎉.

Rustic weddings have a unique charm that appeals to varying tastes😍

They seamlessly adapt to an array of colour schemes, including soft pastels, natural earth tones, and vibrant autumn palettes.

This guide will explore the latest trends in rustic wedding venues, attire, decor, food, and entertainment (a ceilidh obvs😉), complete with inspiring ideas for your stationery.

Rustic Venue Choices 🗺️

Charming Barns and Farms 🚜

Barns and farms offer a quintessential rustic setting with their natural wood structures and rural surroundings.

Enhance these spaces with fairy lights, lanterns, and drapery to add a touch of whimsy and romance💕.

Outdoor Splendour 🌞

For those who prefer an open-air ceremony, consider a forest, a meadow, or a garden.

These natural settings provide a serene and picturesque backdrop.

Add simple decorations like wooden benches or hay bales for seating to maintain the rustic theme.

Incorporate natural materials such as wood, stone, and burlap into your decor. Consider handmade wooden signs for directions, table numbers, or sweet messages. Use burlap for table runners or chair ties, and accent with twine for a cohesive look👍

Homestyle Catering and Refreshments 🍻

Locally Inspired Dining Experience🍾: Prioritise farm-to-table dining by featuring locally sourced, organic ingredients.

Create a communal atmosphere with a buffet-style setup or hire food trucks or a hog roast.

Elevate the ambience with live acoustic music.

Hire bands or musicians to infuse your celebration with a warm and authentic feel🎵.

Engaging Entertainment Choices: Choose interactive activities to enhance the celebration.

From outdoor games to a DIY photo booth📱 with props, or even concluding the evening with a cosy bonfire and marshmallow roasting, these interactive elements ensure a memorable and lively experience for your guests.

And what’s more engaging than a fun, social and inclusive🌈 ceilidh?

Rustic Wedding Decor✨

Environmentally Conscious Wedding Elements: Prioritise sustainability with thoughtfully chosen details♻️

Choose locally sourced flowers and greenery to reduce your carbon footprint.

Consider incorporating potted plants and herbs as eco-friendly wedding favours, allowing guests to take home a piece of your celebration.

Green Lighting Choices:

Illuminate your rustic setting with solar-powered fairy lights and LED candles for a magical ambience that aligns with environmental values💚

Artisanal Decor:

Embrace the trend of DIY decorations, focusing on upcycled and recycled materials.

By incorporating simple, handcrafted or repurposed items🎭, you not only add a personal touch to your wedding but also contribute to a more sustainable celebration.

Best Wedding Attire for Your Rustic Wedding👰👰

Ethical and Sustainable Wedding Dresses:

Ethically sourced materials and sustainable practices in bridal fashion are trending.

Dresses with natural fabrics, floral lace, and comfortable designs are perfect for a rustic theme💛🧡💜

Relaxed Groom Attire:

If you dont fancy getting decked out in a kilt, then a tweed or linen suit in earthy tones is ideal.

Consider accessories like leather suspenders and handmade boutonnieres for a personal touch.

A boutonniere is a single flower🌹 or a small cluster of flowers that is typically reserved for formal occasions, including weddings.

Obvs, you’ll need a hipflask and probably a quaich 😉(pronounced quake btw).

Natural Hair and Makeup 💋:

Embrace a more natural look with minimal makeup and relaxed hairstyles,

complemented by accessories like flower crowns or simple hairpins.

Rustic Wedding Stationery Trends✍️

Sustainable Elegance: Opt for invitations, place cards, and menus crafted from recycled or plantable seed paper,

ensuring a unique touch that aligns with eco-friendly values.

Go Digital📲:

Embrace the digital era by considering electronic save-the-dates or invitations, minimising paper consumption.

Reserve printed wedding stationery exclusively for the actual event if you’d like to cut your carbon emissions even further.

Laser-Cut Designs⚔️:

Add a touch of modern sophistication to your rustic wedding stationery with laser-cut designs.

Intricate patterns on invitations or menu cards can bring a contemporary edge while maintaining the rustic aesthetic.

Watercolour Accents:

Incorporate watercolour elements into your stationery for a blend of modern artistry and rustic charm.

Subtle, pastel hues can elevate the overall visual appeal while being bang on trend for 2024🎨.

So this year, a rustic wedding is defined by the art of personalisation,

a commitment to sustainability, and embracing the beauty of the surroundings around you.

Be it with beautiful flowers, photography and by incorporating these concepts,

your wedding transforms into a genuine celebration of love and reflects your individual styles💝.

A ceilidh (pr. kay-lea) is probably the most inclusive dancefloor you could have

It doesn’t matter how many of them you’ve done before, or if you’ve done none at all.

The person calling the dances tells everyone what they need to do, and keeps it going.

Everyone dances with everyone, whether you know each other or not.

The more people the better! Everyone gets a go. No one has the chance to bust different moves, and because no one *really* knows what’s going on😍and it’s very difficult to feel self-conscious.

Essentially, most people are just guessing what they’re supposed to be doing, and that makes it brilliant.
