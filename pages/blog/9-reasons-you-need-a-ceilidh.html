9 Reasons You Need A Ceilidh Dance At Your Event

Do you love to dance? Want to enjoy an evening of live music 🎵 and dancing with friends, family or colleagues? You should never miss out on a ceilidh (pronounced Kay-lea BTW) dance! Ceilidh dances are traditional social gatherings where people come together for all-night dancing, drinking, storytelling, singing and more. There are many reasons why you should attend a ceilidh dance: to have fun, meet new people or to spend time with old friends. I’ve pulled together 9 reasons you need to attend your next ceilidh dance!

Ceilidhs are fantastic wedding receptions or party entertainment held in celebration of a special occasion. This article will discuss 9 reasons why ceilidhs are so great!

1. It brings your guests together😍

A ceilidh is a social gathering that spreads joy and happiness throughout the room. There is such a sense of collective energy that rises with each dance. All different shapes, sizes, and ages unite in their love of music and dance. It’s not uncommon for cliques to break down at ceilidhs, generating new friendships!

Too often at events, guests like to keep to their friendship circles, however, a ceilidh tends to create a sense of community, encouraging everyone to mingle and get to know each other. This, of course, is a great asset at your event!

2. Something different!

Ceilidhs are more than just a wedding reception or party. They provide your guests with an experience of something different. Ceilidhs are all about having fun, meeting people and connecting with friends old and new. You won’t regret attending your next ceilidh!

People always want something fresh and new at their wedding or party. You can make your wedding reception or even office party more interesting by including a ceilidh dance!

Party formats often follow the same old tired format: Grab a drink, sit at a table, wait for the DJ to play music. You know what wedding receptions or party entertainment are like, right?!

Having a ceilidh at your next function will make your wedding or celebration one of a kind. People with never forget about the ceilidh dance at your wedding!

3. No dance experience is necessary!

You don’t need any dance experience in order to enjoy the ceilidh. It’s all about having fun and enjoying yourself!

My role as your Ceilidh Caller is to guide you through the dance, so really- you don’t need to know (or even remember) what to do🎤 .

Since there are no dance steps to learn, you’ll be able to focus on the music and the movements. The ceilidh is meant to mimic everyday life – that is to say that ceilidhs are very natural dances. People of all ages will feel comfortable dancing at a ceilidh.

Music and dancing are the heart and soul of a ceilidh. The best part? You will never need to dance in front of anyone! Whether you’re a terrible dancer or an experienced one, there is no need to be self-conscious at a ceilidh. With so many people joining in you won’t even know who’s watching you!

4. It brings live music to your event🎵

The ceilidh is all about bringing live music to your event! There are no formalities, no rehearsals. People show up, take a seat and join in the dancing.

Bringing live music to your wedding reception or party can really add a wonderful atmosphere! Live music not only sounds better than recorded music but also creates a sense of liveliness. Being able to dance along to the lively rhythm makes it all the more fun!

The ceilidh band will get your guests up and dancing!

5. Gives Your Guests an opportunity to be involved🥂

A ceilidh is a fantastic opportunity for your guests to participate in the wedding festivities. At many wedding receptions, your guests may find themselves bored as they await their turn at the buffet, but at a ceilidh, not only will they be able to dance and engage in the spirit of the wedding, they’ll also be able to perform traditional ceilidh dances. And when was the last time you could say that?! Too often, guests have little to do at events – they really need a reason to get up and become involved. A ceilidh does just that!

6. You will learn something new about yourself👩‍❤️‍👩

You will learn something new about yourself at a ceilidh! A lot of people think that they’re not good at dancing or that they won’t know how to have fun.

However, the ceilidh is so welcoming, encouraging and accepting that it’s impossible not to have a great time. You’ll be able to forget about your inhibitions and just enjoy the evening.

You never know, you may even discover that you’re not as bad of a dancer as you thought!

7. It will fill a dance floor!

DJ’s are often a gamble. Their playlists are usually too pop-y, too slow or just plain boring. Having a ceilidh is not only fun but it fills the dance floor! With some gentle encouragement from the ceilidh band/caller, you can be sure that whatever their dancing ability, your guests will be out on the dance floor creating an electric party atmosphere.

When people see that the other guests are enjoying themselves they will flock to the dancefloor and enjoy themselves as well.

8. It’s super memorable

At any event, your guests are looking to experience that x-factor that will mark your occasion. A ceilidh band is terrific fun, provides good music, fizzing excitement, liveliness and then some! Your guests will have so much fun on the dancefloor and they won’t forget it in a hurry. It’s these experiences that make wedding receptions and parties more memorable and your guests will thank you for it!

9. Excellent Value🏆

Pound for pound, a ceilidh provides amazing value for money. Not only will you get a wedding reception or party that is off the hook with energy and excitement, but you’ll also get first-class wedding entertainment for hours! It’s also super simple to set up. All we need is a spare corner of a room and we’ll bring the dancefloor to you. Be it a small intimate group or a wedding with hundreds, a ceilidh can accommodate all!

I’ve hosted ceilidhs in all sorts of venues from cosy wee village halls, marquees and tipi’s, right the way through to castles (Newark & Warwick) and even London Zoo.

10. (Bonus!) Fantastic Photo Opportunities!

The ceilidh provides the perfect opportunity to take some really special wedding photos with all your wedding guests. You have no idea how often wedding photos are posed, stiff and formal. This makes for some pretty boring wedding pictures. The ceilidh is so lively, fun and energetic that your photographer will be busy snapping away capturing amazing photos.

Conclusion:

There you go – 9 reasons to have a ceilidh dance at your event! If you want to make your wedding more memorable, give guests an opportunity to be involved in the festivities or if you just need a fun way for people of all backgrounds and skill levels to enjoy themselves while dancing at your next party – then it’s time for a ceilidh. Ceilidhs are super easy to set up, provide great value and let everyone have some wedding day dance floor memories they’ll never forget!
