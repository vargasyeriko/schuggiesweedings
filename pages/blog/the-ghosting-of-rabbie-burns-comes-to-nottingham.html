The Ghosting of Rabbie Burns comes to Nottingham🎭

In this feature Blog, I explore the heartland of British Romanticism, but it’s not just about historical love – it’s about modern love and ghosting!

💔Unravel the 18th Century meets 21st Century comedy-drama, “The Ghosting of Rabbie Burns.”

Discover how the ghost of Scotland’s Bard imparts dating wisdom to a heartbroken author,

blending classic poems with the quirks of contemporary romance.

Whether you’ve found ‘the one’ or are still on the search,

this is your ticket to a historic and ghostly night of entertainment.

Don’t miss the chance to join in on the traditional dancing after the show!
