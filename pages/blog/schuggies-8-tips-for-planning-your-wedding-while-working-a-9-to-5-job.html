Schuggies 8 Tips for Planning Your Wedding While Working a 9-to-5 Job

Planning a wedding is like herding sheep — but when you’ve got a full-time job, it’s more like herding sheep while doing spreadsheets.😅

Below are my refreshed, 2026-ready tips so you can juggle work, life and wedding magic without burning out.

Why This Blog Matters in 2026

Office hours, back-to-back Zooms, email floods — weddings don’t wait for your calendar to clear.

These tips help you plan smart, not hard, so your big day stays fun and relatively (hopefully) stress-free.

🛠️ Steps That Actually Work (Even If You’re Swamped)

1. Launch with a “Vision Day”

Block off one evening or weekend. Get clear on your vibe: colours, venue feels, dancefloor energy. Use that as your north star.

2. Build a Micro-Timeline

Forget exhaustive spreadsheets. Create a simple 12-month and 3-month timeline with must-dates.

Use a shared Google Sheet or app so your partner, vendors, and planner (if you hire one) can peek.

3. Automate, Delegate & Outsource

  • Use templates for emails to vendors, guests, and helpersDelegate tasks (dress hunting, transport, guest tracking) to family/friends
  • Delegate tasks (dress hunting, transport, guest tracking) to family/friends
  • Outsource where possible — e.g. stationery, coordination on wedding day and not just to Auntie Sheila😉

4. Leverage Your Lunchtimes & Commutes🕒

Make calls, send emails, and review quotes during your lunch break or while on public transportation. Little bursts count.

5. Use “Bad Weather” Slots

When lighter tasks (e.g. writing thank-you cards, choosing playlists) don’t need full brain power, slot them into evenings or into “rainy day” windows.🌧️

6. Cluster Tasks Together

Group vendor calls in one go, venue visits on the same day, dress fittings in one week. Efficiency = sanity.

7. Keep a Running “Nice-to-Have” List

If an idea isn’t essential, file it in this list. Return only when your core is solid. (Yes, you’ll drop plenty.)🤯

8. Guard Your Weekends

Reserve at least one weekend day as “No Wedding.” Reboot. Hang with pals, take a walk, binge a series.🍻

Even if your days are full, your wedding planning doesn’t have to feel impossible.

💬 FAQs: Planning a Wedding While Working Full-Time

Can I plan a wedding while working full-time?

The key is breaking tasks into manageable chunks, using planning tools, and setting realistic timelines.

How long does it take to plan a wedding while working 9-to-5?

Most couples manage it comfortably within 12–18 months.

The trick? Prioritise early — book your venue, ceilidh band and caterer first, then fill in the fun bits later.

How do I stay organised during wedding planning?

Use a shared Google Sheet or wedding app, schedule weekly check-ins, and keep a “must-do” versus “nice-to-have” list.

What’s the best time to plan wedding tasks if I’m busy?

Lunchtimes, commutes and early evenings work wonders.

Avoid burnout by keeping weekends for rest and fun.

How do I make wedding planning more fun?

Play music, open a bottle, and celebrate small wins together.

Remember: it’s your love story — not a corporate project!
