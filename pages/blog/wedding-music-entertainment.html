Wedding Music Entertainment

Music🎵 is an integral part of the wedding experience, so choosing the right music for your big day can be a daunting task. There are many different styles of live music to choose from, including jazz bands, string quartets, DJs and more. The type of band or musician you select will depend on your tastes and budget. For some couples it’s important that there be a variety of musical genres played throughout the evening; for others, it may be more important to have live musicians playing their favourite songs at key moments during the ceremony and reception.

One thing you don’t want is the stress most couples experience as they get to the entertainment booking: how can we avoid tumbleweed on the dancefloor🥺.

And that BIG question. Will our guests dance!

What is the best wedding entertainment?

There is no definitive answer to this question. There are a variety of different types of wedding music entertainment that can be perfect for your big day, depending on your preferences and what you’re looking for.

I’ve collated some popular options in this blog- read on…

How much do you pay for wedding entertainment?

Wedding music entertainment can vary in price depending on what you’re looking for. If you want a string quartet, it will be significantly more expensive than hiring a DJ. Some other factors come into play when it comes to pricing, such as the background of the performer/band and how long they have been in business. In general, you can pay as much or as little as you can find but remember, you usually get what you pay for.

What music is usually played at weddings?

Most wedding music entertainment is geared towards listening to a mix of popular love songs and upbeat bangers (!) so your guests can dance and have a good time. Popular jazz standards and classic rock n’ roll and pop songs🎶 are also very common choices.

Keeping the wedding reception fun!

Here are some wonderful wedding entertainment ideas to keep your wedding day full of fun and excitement!

A DJ can help you keep the fun going all night long! You can have your entire wedding day catered to exactly how you want it when hiring a DJ service. The right DJ will be able to read the crowd, playing songs that are appropriate for everyone attending! Keeping the dance floor active

Everybody loves to dance at weddings. But, when you don’t have the right music playing, people are less inclined to get out on the floor

Live Band

Maybe you’re looking for something more than just a DJ. If so, consider a live band to raise the roof at your wedding. They’ll get everyone on their feet, dancing all night long! Live wedding music bands can really set the tone and create an unforgettable atmosphere for your wedding day. Wedding bands typically cost from £1000 upwards. Ideal for the evening reception. And even more immense when you are hosting an outdoor 🌤️wedding.

Harpist (or a clàrsach in Scottish)

There’s something truly magical about a harpist playing at your wedding. The sound of the harp is elegant and graceful, adding a touch of sophistication to your event. A harpist can set the mood for your entire wedding – making it feel more special and romantic. If you’re looking to make your wedding day even more memorable, consider hiring a harpist! Ideal for afternoon drinks reception. Clàrsach, is pronounced clar‧sach btw)

Ceilidh

A ceilidh is a really social party dance that is perfect for a wedding reception! Not only is it a lot of fun, but it’s also a great way to get your guests up and active. With everyone dancing together, it’s the perfect way to create a lively and festive atmosphere at your wedding! Your wedding guests will have a blast which is exactly what you want, right?!

Have you read my blog about using a ceilidh to get out of that first dance stress- Ceilidh Dance – Your First Dance Alternative!

String Quartet

If your wedding is in a more formal setting, then you might want to hire a string quartet for your ceremony. The sound of the quartet will leave everyone with chills! They can also play during the reception, providing a more sophisticated touch to your event.

Roaming musician

For something different at your wedding, consider hiring a roaming musician! This is an excellent way to guarantee that your guests are entertained as they mingle through the reception. A roaming jazz band is a great choice if your performance space is limited.

Silent Disco

Live entertainment isn’t always “live”. If your guests want to dance and you’d rather not hire a band or DJ, you can still make sure people are having fun! A silent disco is a great way to keep people entertained and on the dance floor for hours.

Karaoke🎤

Who doesn’t love a good karaoke session? Karaoke is the perfect way to get everyone in on the fun. It’s also a great way to show off your vocal chops (or lack thereof)! If you’re having a more informal wedding and looking to add some laughs and excitement to your wedding reception, this could be a winner!

Steel Drum Band

A steel drum band is a perfect way to add a touch of island flair to your wedding. With everyone shaking their hips to the beat, a steel drum band is sure to get your guests up and moving!

Guest playlist

If you’re not sure what type of music you want at your wedding, why not let your guests choose? Have your guests send you their top ten favourite songs and hire a band or DJ to play them during your reception. This is also a great way to guarantee that your wedding is full of the music you and your guests love for a great evening party.

Tribute band🎶

A tribute band can bring a touch of energy and excitement to your wedding. If you’ve always wanted to see a certain band play live, but they’re simply too expensive (or no longer together!), consider hiring them as your wedding entertainment! This is a great way to have the music you love without having the entourage and diva demands! If you’re looking for an unforgettable night, consider a tribute band!

Vocalist

Hiring a vocalist for your wedding is a great way to have live music while keeping down your wedding entertainment costs. If you’re looking for an intimate setting, consider booking a vocalist because this is the perfect way to get everyone at your wedding talking. There are many types of vocalists available including opera singers, jazz singers, and even beatboxers! They also make a great choice for your first dance song or indeed, your wedding ceremony whilst you sign your register.

Pianist🎹

A pianist is a perfect way to add a touch of class to your wedding. They can play classical music during your ceremony or reception, or even accompany a vocalist for an extra special touch. If you’re looking for something elegant and timeless, a pianist will be a perfect choice! Ideal for your wedding breakfast.

Classical guitarists🎸

Classical guitarists are a perfect addition to your ceremony and will leave everyone feeling relaxed and calm. By adding classical music to your wedding, you can provide an intimate and romantic atmosphere for the perfect day! Ideal for either the drinks reception or great evening entertainment as your room is turned around. No pa sound system is required and often available as acoustic duos.

Conclusion

There are a lot of great options for wedding music entertainment. You can choose a DJ, live band, string quartet, harpist, steel drum band, or pianist. You can also choose to have karaoke or a silent disco at your wedding. If you want something a little more unique, you could also consider a ceilidh or tribute band. Whatever you decide, make sure that you pick music that will create the right mood for your guests!

Which type of wedding music entertainment is best for you? It really depends on your individual taste and what type of atmosphere you’re hoping to create. No matter what you choose, be sure to have a lot of fun with it! After all, it’s your wedding day – and it should be exactly how you want🧡💙💜
