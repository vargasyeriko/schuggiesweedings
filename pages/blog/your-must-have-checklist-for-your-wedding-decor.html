Your Must Have Checklist For Your Wedding Décor

Designing your wedding can feel a little overwhelming, especially if your venue is a blank slate and gives you free rein. There are so many different décor elements that go into your special day we know it can be difficult to remember everything! This checklist will help you to plan😍 and arrange every detail and whether you decide to include everything or just a few items, it will help you stay on top of your wedding planning game.

Bigger Items🎉

  • Welcome sign
  • Seating plan
  • Aisle runner/markers
  • Order of events
  • Centrepieces
  • Top table
  • Guest tables
  • Tables
  • Chairs
  • Table clothes
  • Chair covers

More Bigger Items✨

  • Order of the Day
  • Lighting
  • Inside
  • Outside
  • Candles – Does the venue have an open flame policy?
  • Faux candles
  • Decorative bottles with fairy lights
  • Flowers
  • Bouquets
  • Boutonnieres Boutonnières are a floral decoration worn by men to accessorise their Morning Suits, Prince Charlie or Argyle jackets for a formal event aka Buttonholes😊
  • Aisle
  • Entry points

Smaller Items 🖋️

  • Reserved seating signs
  • Table numbers
  • Place cards
  • Napkins
  • Favours
  • Guest Book table décor
  • Guest Book and pen
