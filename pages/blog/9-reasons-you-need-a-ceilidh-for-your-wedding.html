9 Reasons You Need A Ceilidh For Your Wedding

Does the thought of a disco fill you with dread?

You deserve a lively atmosphere which is tailored to your guests and will engage with them all.

Do you love to dance? Want to enjoy an evening of live music 🎵 and dancing with your nearest and dearest (family friends and even colleagues)? You should never miss out on a chance to have a ceilidh (pronounced Kay-lea BTW) dance! Ceilidh dances are traditional social gatherings where people come together and everyone gets a go.

No one has the chance to bust different moves, and because no one *really* knows what’s going on it’s very difficult to feel self-conscious.

There are many reasons why you should seriously consider having a ceilidh dance at your wedding. You don’t have to be Scottish or have any Scottish heritage. If you’re looking for fun, social and inclusive entertainment to help you celebrate the biggest day of your lives.

Then a ceilidh is exactly what you are looking for.

In the blog, I’ve pulled together 9 reasons you need to have a ceilidh dance at your wedding!

Ceilidhs are fantastic wedding receptions or wedding entertainment held in celebration of a special occasion.

In this Blog, I’ll highlight 9 reasons why ceilidhs are so great!

1. IT BRINGS YOUR GUESTS TOGETHER😍

A ceilidh is a social gathering that spreads joy and happiness throughout the room. There is such a sense of collective energy that rises with each dance. All different shapes, sizes, and ages unite in their love of music and dance. It’s not uncommon for cliques to break down at ceilidhs, generating new friendships!

Too often at weddings, guests like to keep to their friendship circles, however, a ceilidh tends to create a sense of community, encouraging everyone to mingle and get to know each other. This, of course, is a great asset at your wedding!

2. SOMETHING DIFFERENT! 💬

Ceilidhs are more than just a type of wedding entertainment. They provide your guests with an experience of something different. Ceilidhs are all about having fun, meeting people and connecting with friends old and new. You won’t regret having ceilidh for your wedding!

People always want something fresh and memorable for their wedding. Make your wedding reception more interesting by including a ceilidh dance!

Wedding formats often follow the same old tired format: Grab a drink, sit at a table, and wait for the DJ to play music. You know what wedding receptions are like, right?! 🤯

Having a ceilidh will make your wedding a one-of-a-kind, that your guest will rave about for years to come.

Your guests will forget the food, and what was said during the speeches, but they won’t forget how your wedding made them feel.

That’s the power of having a proper social ceilidh.

3. NO DANCE EXPERIENCE IS NECESSARY! ✨

You don’t need any dance experience to enjoy the ceilidh. It’s all about having fun and enjoying yourself! Making mistakes are half the fun- essentially, most people are just guessing what they’re supposed to be doing, and that makes it brilliant.

My role as your Ceilidh Caller is to guide everyone through the dances, so really- you don’t need to know (or even remember) what to do🎤.

Since there are no dance steps to learn, you’ll be able to focus on the music and the movements. The Ceilidh is meant to mimic everyday life – that is to say that ceilidhs are very natural dances. People of all ages will feel comfortable dancing at a ceilidh.

Music and dancing are the heart and soul of a ceilidh. The best part? You will never need to dance in front of anyone! Whether you’re a terrible dancer or an experienced one, there is no need to be self-conscious at a ceilidh. With so many people joining in you won’t even know who’s watching you!

4. IT BRINGS LIVE MUSIC TO YOUR WEDDING🎵

The Ceilidh is all about bringing music to your wedding! Whether that’s a DJ-style set up where venue space is limited or a full-on live ceilidh band, there are no formalities, no rehearsals.

Even your evening guest, join in and quickly become part of your celebration💜.

Bringing this sort of music to your wedding reception adds a wonderful atmosphere!

Ceilidh’s music not only sounds better but also creates a sense of liveliness. Being able to dance along to the lively rhythm makes it all the more fun!

5. Gives Your Guests an opportunity to be involved🥂

A ceilidh is a fantastic opportunity for your guests to participate in the wedding festivities. At many weddings, your guests may find themselves bored as they await their turn at the buffet! But not at a ceilidh. Not only will they be able to dance and engage in the spirit of the wedding, but they’ll also be able to perform authentic contemporary ceilidh dances. And when was the last time you could say that?!

Too often, guests have little to do at weddings – they really need a reason to get up and become involved. A ceilidh does just that!

6. You will learn something new about yourself👩‍❤️‍👩

You will learn something new about yourself at a ceilidh! A lot of people think that they’re not good at dancing or that they won’t know how to have fun.

However, the ceilidh is so welcoming, encouraging and accepting that it’s impossible not to have a great time. Your guests are able to forget about their inhibitions and just enjoy the evening. If they still need some encouragement, that’s what your ceilidh caller is for🎉

You never know, they may even discover that they’re not as bad of a dancer as they thought!

7. It will fill a dance floor!🤟

DJs are often a gamble. Their playlists are usually too pop-y, too slow or just plain boring. Having a ceilidh is not only fun but it fills the dance floor! With some gentle encouragement from the ceilidh band/caller, you can be sure that whatever their dancing ability, your guests will be out on the dance floor creating an electric party atmosphere.

When people see that the other guests are enjoying themselves they will flock to the dancefloor and enjoy themselves as well.

8. It’s super memorable🎉

At any wedding, your guests are looking to experience that x-factor that will mark your occasion. A ceilidh is terrific fun, provides good music, fizzing excitement, liveliness and then some! Your guests will have so much fun on the dancefloor and they won’t forget it in a hurry. It’s these experiences that make your wedding reception more memorable and your guests will thank you for it!

9. Excellent Value🏆

Pound for pound, a ceilidh provides amazing value for money. Not only will you get a wedding reception that is off the hook with energy and excitement, but you’ll also get first-class entertainment for hours! It’s also super simple to set up. All we need is a spare corner of a room and we’ll bring the dancefloor to you. Be it a small intimate group or a wedding with hundreds, a ceilidh can accommodate all!

I’ve hosted ceilidhs in all sorts of venues from cosy wee village halls, marquees and tipis, right the way through to castles (Newark & Warwick) and even London Zoo.

Having packages that work for sorts of spaces means you don’t have to compromise on the entertainment when you have the venue of your dreams💜.

10. (Bonus!) Fantastic Photo Opportunities!📸

The ceilidh provides the perfect opportunity to take some really special wedding photos with all your guests. You have no idea how often wedding photos are posed, stiff and formal. This makes for some pretty boring pictures. The Ceilidh is so lively, fun and energetic that your photographer will be busy snapping away and capturing amazing photos🍾.

CONCLUSION:

There you go – 10 reasons (with the bonus one🥰) to have a ceilidh dance at your wedding! If you want to make your wedding even more memorable. Give your guests the opportunity to be involved in the festivities in a fun way that folks from all backgrounds and parts of your lives can enjoy– then it’s time for a ceilidh. Ceilidhs are super easy to set up, provide great value and let everyone have some wedding day dance floor memories they’ll never forget!
