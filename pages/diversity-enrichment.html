Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

Ceilidh dancing brings mixed groups together fast — which makes it a genuinely useful inclusion tool, not a box-ticking exercise.

To make the world more awesome, one ceilidh dance at a time.

Everyone can join in

If you're worried you can't be included in a ceilidh dance, think again. It doesn't matter what your level of ability is, whether you're able-bodied or have a learning disability — ceilidh dancing adapts to the people in the room.

Culture, not just Scottish culture

Ceilidhs are a great way to experience and promote culture — and not only Scottish culture. The style can be tailored to your group and your occasion.
