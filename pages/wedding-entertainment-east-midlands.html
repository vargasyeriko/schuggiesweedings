What does the call bit mean exactly?

Well, I’m a bit like an MC/Toastmaster and conductor all rolled into one🎤. I gently encourage your guests up onto the floor- walk them through the dances and then call out what they need to do as they dance- so they don’t need to know what they’re doing. Actually, mistakes are half the fun of ceilidh dances.

This is breaking the ice between the types of guests that might not know each other such as different sides of your families, your friends and even work colleagues. Sounds a bit like Tinder, but it’s much more fun than that.

As the Caller, I also make sure your evening runs on time, so breaks for food, sparklers and evening photos if you are having them.

If you are leaving early; I round up the troops to bid you a fond farewell 👋and I make you’re your guests know it’s last orders at the bar when that time comes🥂.

All this means that you get to relax, socialise and enjoy the evening celebrations. Don’t just take my word for it, you can read my testimonials on Google My Business and @WeddingCeilidhs on Facebook. If you need more social proof, why not follow me on Insta, Facebook and even TikTok.
