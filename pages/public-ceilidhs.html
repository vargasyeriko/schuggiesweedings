Nottingham Ceilidh Club

What a club night is like

  • No experience neededEvery dance is walked through and called. Beginners are the norm, not the exception.
  • No partner, no membershipCome on your own — there are always friendly folk happy to give you a hand.
  • Live or recorded musicClub nights run to a mix of both, depending on the evening.
  • Called gender-freeEveryone dances every part. No "ladies" and "gents" — just dancers.
  • Schuggie usually callsAnd guest callers are always made very welcome.
  • Spiritual homeThe Boat & Horses, Beeston Rylands — with other venues in the mix.
