What I do

Whatever the event — a wedding, birthday, social occasion or fundraiser — you want it hosted professionally so you can relax and enjoy the night.

I host the ceilidh for you: calling the dances and MC'ing your evening so you can properly relax — after all, it's your party! I get everyone together for the first dance, keep them on the floor, coordinate with caterers around food, and make sure moments like the bouquet toss, sparklers or Auld Lang Syne all flow seamlessly.

Calling is simply directing the dancing — I'm the person who does it. Most of your guests will never have been to a ceilidh, so I guide them through each move (left, right, hop, and so on), which means nobody needs any experience.

Half the fun is making mistakes — they're actively encouraged, nobody feels self-conscious, and there's no pressure to get it right. The other half is just having a brilliant time.

Ceilidh dancing isn't so technical that you need teaching — it's all about being fun, social and inclusive. If you did anything like this at school, my style is a lot more fun and a lot less stressful.
